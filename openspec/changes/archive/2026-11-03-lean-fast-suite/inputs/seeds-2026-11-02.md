## Redundancy evidence (coverage set differences)

- **`tests/unit/service_layer` beyond `tests/bdd`**: 75 lines and 26 branches of
  `fingerprint/service_layer/` — `collection.py` 36 l / 4 b, `representations.py` 11 / 6,
  `settings.py` 11 / 2, `generators/shacl.py` 6 / 6, `generators/generator.py` 5 / 3,
  `plantuml.py`, `handlers.py`, `prefixes.py` 2 each. Everything else they cover, BDD covers too.
- **`tests/bdd` beyond all of `tests/unit` (no Hypothesis)**: 216 lines / 77 branches, chiefly
  `fingerprint/domain/patterns.py` (84 l / 66 b) and the generated `views.py` (108 l). In the fast
  gate, `patterns.py` is proven only through BDD; its unit tests in `tests/unit/domain/test_patterns.py`
  are Hypothesis properties, which the fast gate skips.
- **Duplicate check**: "Shapes report no violation on their own data" is a spec scenario with its
  Gherkin scenario in `tests/features/profile_representations.feature`, and is tested again under
  the same name in `tests/unit/service_layer/test_representations_golden.py`, parametrised over
  3 graphs × 3 thresholds (9 tests; the 3 treaties cases cost 56 s with coverage).
- **Pitfall BDD scenarios** cost ~2 s each: each runs the packaged check catalogue as SPARQL over
  `FakeSparql` (rdflib in memory), whatever the scenario asserts.

## Prior decisions this builds on

The archived `openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-01-lean-test-suite/`: DEC-1 (spec scenarios traced
by title), DEC-2 / design P-2 (one owning tier per check: behaviour → `tests/bdd`, branch/error
paths no scenario reaches → `tests/unit/<layer>`, all-inputs rules → domain Hypothesis properties),
design P-6 (redundancy removal: delete, keep only if branch coverage and the killed-mutant set are
unchanged). The service-layer unit tier was not fully pruned under P-6.
