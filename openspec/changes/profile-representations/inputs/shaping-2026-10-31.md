| 8 | Several graphs? | **One SHACL and one PlantUML file per graph; one HTML document** with a chapter per graph. |

## Calls made by the agent (developer may override)

- SHACL shapes repeat inherited properties (validation does not depend on inference); the diagram
  shows generalisations (declared solid, implied dashed) and only own/refined properties per class.
- Shape IRIs are minted under a `shapes_namespace` setting, defaulting to a `urn:` namespace.
- The saved profile is the **raw run** (`ProfileRun`), not the analytics: generators re-derive the
  analytics on load, so a threshold can change without collecting again.
- Overlap with `llm-schema-tools` (its draft item 2): this EPIC owns the generator framework and
  these three formats; VoID, JSON-as-representation and LLM schema cards stay out and can be added
  as further generators by that EPIC.
